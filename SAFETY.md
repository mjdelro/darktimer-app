# Safety and accuracy

darkTimer is a general-purpose timing and calculation aid. It is not a
substitute for the current product label, safety data sheet, manufacturer
technical publication, training, or professional advice.

- Verify the film, developer, dilution, temperature, agitation, and time before
  processing important negatives.
- Follow the current label and safety data sheet for protective equipment,
  ventilation, mixing order, storage, first aid, incompatibilities, and disposal.
- Never mix chemicals unless the manufacturer instructions expressly direct it.
- Keep photographic chemistry away from children, pets, food, and drink.
- Follow local rules for wastewater and hazardous-waste disposal.
- Adjust fixing time for the product, film, dilution, temperature, and fixer
  condition. Use a clearing test when the manufacturer recommends one.

Times and calculated volumes are provided without a guarantee of a particular
photographic, archival, health, safety, or environmental result. Product names
are used for identification only. darkTimer is independent and is not affiliated
with or endorsed by the named manufacturers.
